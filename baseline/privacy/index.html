Baseline privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Baseline turns the Apple Health data on your iPhone into daily scores. It has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics.

What Baseline reads

With your permission, Baseline reads from Apple Health: sleep; heart rate, including resting and walking heart rate, heart rate recovery, heart rate variability and the heartbeat readings it is calculated from; breathing rate and breathing disturbances; blood oxygen; wrist temperature; VO₂ max; activity (steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, stand hours, distance, walking and running speed, Activity rings); workouts, with their routes, how hard they felt and the weather saved with them; and your date of birth and sex (for heart rate zones and bio age). A workout's route is read only while you look at that workout, drawn on your iPhone over an Apple Maps map (Apple Maps loads the map of that area), and never stored or sent anywhere. It never writes to Apple Health. You can change what it reads at any time in the Apple Health app: tap your picture, then Apps.

Where your data stays

Scores are worked out on your iPhone and kept there in Baseline's own storage, with a copy of the last 24 weeks of Apple Health readings they come from, your answers to the morning questions and a log of recent syncs.

Your Apple Watch and Baseline's widgets get copies of your scores and of the readings they show, such as last night's sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, and today's workouts. Once you let Baseline add workouts, it keeps your week's guided sessions, with your heart rate ranges, in your Apple Watch's Workout app.

Nothing is sent to us. Baseline doesn't use iCloud. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, the backup holds Baseline's files, as it does other apps'.

Settings › Apple Health can share a log of recent syncs and, for people testing the app, their check-in answers. Neither goes to anyone unless you share it.

Deleting Baseline deletes its storage on your iPhone and its Apple Watch app.

What we never do

Baseline does not sell your data, share it with anyone, use it for advertising, marketing or data mining, or track you across apps and websites.

Not medical advice

Baseline compares your readings with your own usual. Its bio age compares them with large published studies. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything. Its guided sessions are suggestions: slow down or stop if you feel dizzy or unwell.
