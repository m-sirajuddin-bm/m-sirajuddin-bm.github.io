Baseline privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Baseline turns the Apple Health data on your iPhone into daily scores. It has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics.

What Baseline reads

With your permission, Baseline reads from Apple Health: sleep; heart rate, including resting and walking heart rate, heart rate recovery, heart rate variability and the heartbeat readings it is calculated from; breathing rate and breathing disturbances; blood oxygen; wrist temperature; VO₂ max; activity (steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, stand hours, distance, time in daylight, Activity rings); workouts, with their routes, how hard they felt and the weather saved with them; and your date of birth (for heart rate zones). A workout's route is read only while you look at that workout, drawn on a map on your iPhone, and never stored or sent anywhere. It never writes to Apple Health. You can change what it reads at any time in the Health app under Sharing.

Where your data stays

Scores are worked out on your iPhone and stored on it, in Baseline's own storage. Your Apple Watch and Baseline's widgets get copies of your scores from the iPhone. Guided sessions you send to your Apple Watch go to its Workout app. Nothing is stored in iCloud and nothing is sent to us. Your answers to the morning questions stay on the iPhone too. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Notes written by AI (optional)

By default, notes are written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model, or shown as plain sentences, and nothing leaves the phone.

If you choose OpenAI in Settings and enter your own OpenAI API key, Baseline sends short fact lines worked out from your data (for example your Recovery score and how it compares with your usual) to OpenAI to write the notes. No raw Health data is sent, and nothing that names you. Your key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain. OpenAI handles that text under its own terms and privacy policy, under your own OpenAI account. You can switch back to the iPhone's notes at any time, and nothing more is sent.

What we never do

Baseline does not sell your data, share it with anyone (apart from the optional OpenAI notes you turn on), use it for advertising, marketing or data mining, or track you across apps and websites.

Not medical advice

Baseline compares your readings with your own usual. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.
